## ⚠️ Important Notes

### What Automated Services CAN'T Do:
- Verify business information with government agencies
- Create high-quality content for each directory
- Respond to customer reviews
- Build relationships with local chambers of commerce
- Ensure 100% accuracy across all platforms

### What You Still Need to Do Manually:
1. **Verify all submissions** - Check that information is correct
2. **Respond to reviews** - Engage with customers
3. **Update content** - Keep portfolio and photos current
4. **Monitor performance** - Track which directories generate leads
5. **Government registrations** - COA, IIA, DDA compliance
