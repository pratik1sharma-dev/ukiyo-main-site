# 🏢 Local Citations Setup Guide for Ukiyo Habitat

## 📍 What are Local Citations?

Local citations are online mentions of your business's **Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP)** on various websites, directories, and platforms. They're essential for local SEO and help your architectural firm appear in local search results.

## 🎯 Why Local Citations Matter for Ukiyo Habitat

- **Local Search Rankings**: Rank higher for "architectural firms in Delhi"
- **Google My Business Support**: Citations strengthen your Google Business Profile
- **Trust & Credibility**: Multiple listings build trust with potential clients
- **Local Visibility**: Appear in local business directories and maps
- **Lead Generation**: Direct traffic from directory listings
